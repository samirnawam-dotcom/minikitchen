Before the shoot

Ground rules

The website is light, calm and photo-led: white and ivory backgrounds with gold details. Photos should feel the same: bright, natural, generous, and real.

Light & style

  • Natural daylight or warm event light
  • Light, airy tones; white linen, flowers, silver and gold
  • Real Mini Kitchen food, set-ups and team

Shoot both ways

  • Every scene in landscape and portrait
  • Leave space around the subject; the site crops
  • Keep the main subject near the centre

Files

  • JPG, sRGB colour, highest quality
  • At least the size listed for each photo
  • Edited (colour, straightened), not filtered

Avoid

  • Text, logos, watermarks or frames on the photo
  • Heavy filters, dark moody edits, harsh flash
  • Photos sent through WhatsApp (it shrinks them)

People

  • No guest faces without their permission
  • Prefer hands, chefs at work, guests softly blurred
  • Team portraits only with the person's OK

Text sits on top

Large headers have white text over the photo, on the left in English and on the right in Arabic. Keep the middle of the photo calm and avoid important details at the far edges.

File names

How to name every file

section-slot-number-short-description.jpg
  • Lowercase English letters, numbers and hyphens only: no spaces, no Arabic, no capitals.
  • Numbers have two digits: 01, 02 … 12.
  • The description is 2–5 words saying what is in the photo.
  • Alternatives of the same shot: add -alt1, -alt2 at the end.

For the photographer

Uploading in the Back Office

If you were given a Back Office login, you can put photos on the website yourself. Only change photos: leave prices, texts, orders and settings as they are. Still send every full-size original to the shared folder above, because some places (catering type cards, page headers, live stations, course close-ups) are placed by the developer, not in the Back Office.

1. Sign in

  1. Open minikitchencatering.com/admin on a computer (easier than a phone for this work).
  2. Sign in with the username and password the owner gave you.
  3. The menu on the left lists the sections. You only need Photos & team, Catering packages and Menu.

2. How every upload works

  1. Next to each photo field there is an upload button (cloud icon). Press it and choose the file.
  2. Wait for “Photo uploaded”. The field now holds the new photo's link.
  3. Press the card's Save button. Nothing changes on the website until you press Save.
  4. Open the website in another tab and refresh to check it.
  • JPG, PNG or WebP. Large camera files are fine: the Back Office shrinks each photo to 2400 px before uploading. iPhone HEIC photos must be saved as JPG first.
  • The website makes the phone and desktop sizes automatically, so upload one file per photo.
  • A card shows “Unsaved changes” until it is saved. Save one card before moving to the next.

Please don't

  • Delete cards, dishes or packages (the bin and Delete buttons)
  • Change prices, names, texts or the Show / Hide switch
  • Open Orders, Settings or Account & backups

If something looks wrong

  • A photo you replaced looks cropped badly: upload a version with more space around the subject
  • You changed something by mistake: don't press Save, just refresh the page
  • Tell the owner which card you changed and when
