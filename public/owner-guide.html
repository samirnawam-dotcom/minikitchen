Your daily routine

Website orders come to you on WhatsApp and are also saved in the Back Office, so nothing gets lost.

  1. Open the Back OfficeSign in at the Back Office address. Tick “Keep session active on this device” on your own phone or computer only.
  2. Check new ordersThe red number next to Orders shows how many are waiting. A chime plays when a new one arrives while the page is open.
  3. Confirm with the customerPress Reply on WhatsApp on the order, agree the details, then set its status to Confirmed.
  4. Send it to the kitchenThe kitchen keeps Kitchen open on a tablet and moves each ticket along: Start Preparing, then Out for Delivery.
  5. Close the dayMark finished orders Completed. Use Export CSV in Orders when you want them in Excel.

Orders and quotes

Every website order gets a reference number such as MK-1005. The customer can use it in the website's order tracker to see the status you set.

New / Pending→ Confirmed→ Preparing in Kitchen→ Out for Delivery→ Completed

Use Cancelled for orders that will not go ahead. The Back Office records four kinds of request:

Type Comes from What to do
Delivery Cart Order A customer's basket on the Delivery or Restaurant menu Confirm the address and delivery zone, then prepare.
Catering & Event Quote The quote form or event calculator Reply with a price. Proposal / PDF prepares a printable proposal.
Restaurant Table Booking The reservation button on the Restaurant page Confirm the time and number of guests.
WhatsApp Lead Any other WhatsApp button on the site Answer the question; no order may follow.
Phone or walk-in order? Press Log Manual Order / Quote at the top of the Back Office so it appears in the same list and on the kitchen display.

Common tasks

Change a price

Menu
  1. Search for the dish.
  2. Type the new price in Price (USD $).
  3. Press Save on that row. The website updates straight away.

Mark a dish sold out

Menu
  1. Find the dish.
  2. Set Stock Status to Sold Out and press Save. Customers see it greyed out and cannot add it.
  3. Set it back to In Stock when it is available again.

Add a new dish

Menu
  1. Press Add New Dish.
  2. Choose the category, enter the name, description and price, and upload a photo (JPG or PNG, under 6 MB).
  3. Press Add Dish to Menu.

Update the dollar rate

Dashboard
  1. In 1 USD = type the new rate in L.L.
  2. Press Update Rate. All LBP prices on the website change.

Change phone numbers or hours

Settings
  1. Edit the WhatsApp numbers, address or opening hours.
  2. Press Save All Website Settings at the bottom.

Add a customer review

Reviews
  1. Press + Add Review.
  2. Enter the name, event, words and star rating.
  3. Save. It appears in the reviews section of the website.

Print table QR codes

QR Codes
  1. Choose the menu, or the numbered table codes for the terrace.
  2. Press Print QR Standee.
  3. Guests who scan a table code see “You are seated at Table #” and a Call Waiter button.

Change a photo

Photos & Team
  1. Open the gallery or team section.
  2. Upload the new photo or paste its link.
  3. Press Save.

What customers see

  • Six pages: Catering, Menus & Packages, Restaurant, Delivery, About Us and Contact.
  • A basket on the menus. “Send Order to WhatsApp” opens WhatsApp with the full order written out, and saves it in your Back Office.
  • An Arabic button (العربية) that switches the layout to right-to-left, and a light or dark mode.
  • An order tracker where they enter their MK- number to see its status.
  • QR codes are only in the Back Office. Customers never see the QR printing screen.

Keeping it safe

The Back Office holds your customers' names, phone numbers and addresses. Treat its password like a bank password.
  • Password: at least 10 characters, not used anywhere else. Change it in Account. Changing it signs out every other device.
  • Sign out with Lock when you use a shared computer.
  • Automatic backups: the server copies the database every day and keeps the last 14 days.
  • Your own copy: once a month, press Download Full Database Backup (.json) in Account and keep the file somewhere safe. Restore Backup (.json) puts it back if ever needed.
  • The activity log in the same tab shows every sign-in, failed sign-in and change, with the time.

When something goes wrong

What you see What to do
The site still shows old prices or layout Press Ctrl + F5 (on a phone, close and reopen the browser tab). Your browser kept an old copy.
The Back Office asks you to sign in again Your session ended. Sign in again; nothing was lost.
“Too many sign-in attempts” Wait 15 minutes, then try again with the correct password.
You forgot the password Ask your website developer to reset it. Nobody can read the old one, it is stored encrypted.
A change shows a red error The change was not saved. Check your internet connection, sign in again and repeat it.
A customer says they ordered but you see nothing Check WhatsApp first: the order message goes there even if their connection dropped before the Back Office saved it.
The website shows “Server Error 500” Contact your website developer with the time it happened.
